10.2 What a Renter pays

A Renter pays the rental price for the booked dates, plus 13% HST. That is all. There is no separate service fee charged to Renters, and there is no protection or insurance fee.

HST is charged on the rental price only. It is not charged on the security deposit, which is a hold rather than a sale.

Separately, a security deposit in the amount set by the Owner is authorized (held) against the Renter's payment method. A deposit hold is not a charge, but it does reduce your available credit until it is released.

Zoink charges a commission to the Owner only. It is deducted from the Owner's payout, and it is calculated on the rental price, based on the listing's daily rate:

Commission is charged on the rental price only. It is never charged on a security deposit or on damage compensation.

11. Security deposits and damage claims

A security deposit is authorized at booking and can end up in one of three states:

  • Held — the authorization is in place and unresolved.
  • Returned to the Renter — released automatically once the rental completes and the 24-hour hold window passes with no claim, or released because a claim was resolved in the Renter's favour.
  • Released to the Owner — captured and paid to the Owner as compensation for an approved damage or non-return claim.

To make a claim, an Owner must open a dispute in the app within 24 hours of the booking completing. After that the window closes and the deposit is released. A claim must include evidence — the handoff photos, the return photos, repair estimates, and anything else relevant. The Renter is given an opportunity to respond.

How Zoink decides. Zoink reviews the evidence and decides how the deposit is allocated. Our decision is final as to the deposit only. It is an administrative allocation of funds we are holding — it is not arbitration, not a legal judgment, and it does not decide who is legally liable. Either party remains free to pursue the other in the Ontario Small Claims Court or any other forum for anything beyond the deposit. We are not obliged to mediate every dispute, and we are not liable for the outcome of one we do mediate.

Damage compensation paid to an Owner is not commissioned and is transferred separately from the rental payout.

Read this section carefully, because it is the one most likely to surprise you.

Zoink does not provide insurance, damage protection, or any guarantee of any kind. We are not an insurer, we do not offer a protection plan, and we do not cover loss, theft, or damage.

The security deposit is the only financial protection in the system, and only if the Owner set one. If an item is worth $2,000 and the deposit is $100, the most that can be recovered through Zoink is $100. Anything beyond that is a matter between the Owner and the Renter, and is for the courts, not for us.

What this means in practice:

  • Owners: set a deposit that reflects what you would actually lose. Do not list anything you cannot afford to have damaged or not returned. Check whether your own home, tenant, or contents insurance covers items you lend out — many policies do not.
  • Renters: you are personally responsible for loss, theft, or damage up to the item's stated value, not just up to the deposit. Check whether your own insurance covers property in your care.
