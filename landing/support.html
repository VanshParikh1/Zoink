Zoink support

Last updated November 2, 2026

Contact us

Email zoinksupport@gmail.com. Write from the student email on your account and, if it's about a rental, include the item and the dates. Reports sent from the app are reviewed within 24 hours.

Common questions

Who can join?

Students with a university email address from a supported Ontario school. You sign up with that address and confirm it with a 6-digit code we email you.

I didn't get my code.

Check your spam or junk folder, then tap resend on the code screen. School mail filters can delay it by a few minutes. If it still doesn't arrive, email us from that address.

How does paying work?

You pay in the app through Stripe. Zoink never sees or stores your card details. The lender gets the rental price minus Zoink's commission, which is 15% of the rental price (minimum $2).

What's a deposit?

A lender can choose to hold a deposit. It's held on your card when you pay, not charged, and released 24 hours after the item is returned unless the lender opens a dispute. Bookings with a deposit can be up to 3 days long and can be paid from the day before they start.

How do pickup and return work?

Meet on campus. At pickup the lender takes photos of the item, then you both tap Zoink It within 5 minutes. At return the renter takes the photos and you both tap again. Pickup can only go ahead once the renter has paid.

Something went wrong with a rental.

Open the booking in the app to raise a dispute, or email us with the booking details. Handoff photos are kept as evidence for both sides.

How do I report or block someone?

On a listing or profile, use the menu to report the listing or the user, or to block them. In a chat, long-press a message to report it.

How do I delete my account?

Profile › Settings › Delete account. See Delete your account for what is removed and what is kept.
