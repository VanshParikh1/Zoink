Delete your Zoink account

Last updated October 30, 2026

Zoink lets students safely rent from and lend to each other on campus. You can delete your account at any time, from inside the app, without contacting us.

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Zoink and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion takes effect straight away and you are signed out. It cannot be undone; if you want to use Zoink again later, register again with your student email.

Before you delete, finish or cancel any booking that is still in progress, and wait for any open dispute to be resolved. Payouts that are still on their way to you go to your Stripe account as normal.

2. Can't open the app?

Email zoinksupport@gmail.com from the student email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Zoink account". We will confirm the request with you and delete the account within 30 days.

3. What is removed

As soon as you delete your account:

  • Your name, phone number, bio and profile photo are removed. Your profile photo file is deleted from our image host.
  • Your email address is replaced with a placeholder, so it is no longer linked to Zoink and you can register with it again.
  • Your password is replaced with a random value, so nobody can sign in to the account again.
  • Your push notification token is removed, so this device stops getting Zoink notifications.
  • Your listings are taken down and can no longer be found or booked.

Anything still left on your account is shown as "Deleted User".

4. What is kept, and for how long

Some records have to outlive your account, for tax law, for the other person in a rental, or to handle disputes. They stay attached to "Deleted User", not to your name, and are deleted or anonymised when their period ends:

RecordKept forWhy
Bookings, payments, commission, payouts and tax records7 years from the end of the tax yearCanadian tax and accounting rules
Handoff and condition photos from your rentals12 months after the rental, or 90 days after a dispute about it is resolved, whichever is laterEvidence if the other person raises a problem
Messages you sent24 months after the conversation's last booking completesThey stay in the other person's conversation
Reviews you wroteIndefinitely, shown as from a deleted userThey are part of the other person's public reputation
Disputes and reports3 years after they are resolvedDetecting repeat abuse, and legal defence
The text and pickup city of a listing someone messaged you about or bookedAs long as those conversations and bookings are kept (above)So the other person can still see what was arranged
Error and diagnostic logs90 daysFixing bugs

Payment card details are never stored by Zoink; they are held by Stripe. If you received payouts, your Stripe Connect account is held by Stripe under its own terms. You can close it with Stripe directly.

The full details are in section 7 ("How long we keep it") and section 8 ("Deleting your account") of our Privacy Policy.
