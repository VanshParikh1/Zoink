The rental flow, minus the chaos.

Group-chat rentals have no answer for "who holds the money?" or "did the handoff actually happen?" Zoink does, at every step.

  1. 01

    Find it nearby.

    Browse by category and distance, then message the owner in the app to sort out dates and a meeting spot.

  2. 02

    Request, then pay.

    Ask for up to 7 days. Once the owner accepts, pay the rent plus 13% HST through Stripe, and the owner's deposit is held on your card.

  3. 03

    Zoink It at pickup.

    Take photos of the item, then both tap Zoink It within five minutes. Only then is the rent actually charged.

  4. 04

    Return and release.

    Photos and Zoink It again at return. If nobody opens a claim within 24 hours, the deposit hold is released and the owner is paid.

The deposit is held, not charged.

Owners set a deposit on each listing. It's authorized on the renter's card when they pay and held through the whole rental. It ends up in one of three places:

  1. During the rental

    Held on the renter's card

    Not a charge, just a hold. It stays in place from payment until 24 hours after the return.

  2. Item comes back fine

    Released to the renter

    Automatically, 24 hours after the return Zoink It, if nobody has opened a claim.

  3. Damage or no return

    Paid to the owner

    The owner opens a claim within 24 hours with the handoff photos. Whatever Zoink approves, up to the full deposit, goes to the owner with no commission taken.

Worth knowing: the deposit is the only money Zoink can recover for an owner, and only if one was set. Zoink doesn't sell insurance. Set a deposit that covers what you'd actually lose. Read the deposit rules.

Questions, answered.

The short version. The Terms have the full rules.

Who can use Zoink?

Students 18 or older who live in Ontario and are enrolled at one of the universities above. You confirm your student email with a one-time code before you can list or book.

What does a renter pay?

The rental price plus 13% HST. There's no service fee and no protection fee. The owner's deposit is held on your card on top of that, but it isn't charged unless a damage claim against it is approved.

When is my card charged?

You pay after the owner accepts, but the rent is only charged once you both Zoink It at pickup. The deposit stays a hold until 24 hours after the return.

How long can I rent something?

Up to 7 days per booking.

What if my item comes back damaged?

Open a claim in the app within 24 hours of the return, with the handoff photos and anything else that helps. The renter gets to respond, then Zoink decides how the deposit is split. An approved amount is paid to you with no commission.

Zoink can only pay out up to the deposit. Anything beyond that is between you and the renter.

Is there insurance?

No. Zoink doesn't offer insurance or a protection plan. The deposit is the only money held against loss or damage, so owners should set one that reflects the item's real value.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Either side can cancel a confirmed booking in the app, free, any time before the rental start time. Everything held on the renter's card is released. After the start time it can't be cancelled.

How much does Zoink take from owners?

15% of the rent for items up to $20/day, 12.5% up to $50/day, and 10% above that. Nothing is taken from deposits or damage payments. You see your exact payout before you accept.
