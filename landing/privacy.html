1. Introduction

Zoink is a peer-to-peer rental marketplace operated as a sole proprietorship by Vansh Parikh in Ontario, Canada.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. It applies to the Zoink mobile app, the Zoink website, and everything connected to them.

We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Zoink currently operates only in Ontario and only for users aged 18 and over.

Privacy contact: zoinksupport@gmail.com. That address reaches the person accountable for privacy at Zoink: Vansh Parikh, 2 Goderich Drive, Brampton, Ontario L7A5A7.

3. Why we use it

We use personal information to:

  1. create, verify, and secure your account;
  2. publish listings and let people find and book them;
  3. process payments, hold and release security deposits, calculate commission, and send payouts;
  4. enable messaging between the two parties to a rental;
  5. send you transactional notifications you have not switched off — booking activity, payments and payouts, deposit updates, messages, and reviews;
  6. investigate reports, resolve disputes, and allocate security deposits;
  7. detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unlawful use;
  8. fix bugs, monitor errors, and improve the product;
  9. meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not use your personal information for any new purpose without telling you and, where the law requires it, getting your consent.

4. Your consent

By creating an account you consent to our collecting and using your personal information as described here.

Some collection is required to provide the service at all — you cannot rent on Zoink without an account, a verified student email, and a payment method. Other collection is optional, and you control it:

  • Location — grant or revoke in your device settings.
  • Camera and photo library — grant or revoke in your device settings.
  • Push notifications by category — toggle in the app's notification settings. Account-critical notices (for example, the outcome of your student email verification) are always sent.

You may withdraw consent to non-essential processing at any time by writing to zoinksupport@gmail.com. Withdrawing consent to essential processing means closing your account.

6. Where your information goes

Zoink uses service providers located outside Canada, primarily in the United States (Stripe, Cloudinary, AWS, Expo, Sentry, and Railway all process data in the US).

This means your personal information may be stored or processed in the United States, and while it is there it may be accessible to US courts, law enforcement, and national security authorities under US law, regardless of the protections we have in place. We use providers who contractually commit to a comparable level of protection, but we cannot exempt them from the law where they operate.

If you would rather your information not be processed outside Canada, do not use Zoink.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app or by emailing zoinksupport@gmail.com.

Some things survive deletion, and you should know which:

  • Transaction records stay for 7 years for tax and accounting.
  • Reviews you wrote about other people stay visible, attributed to a deleted user.
  • Messages you sent stay in the other party's conversation view.
  • An open dispute or booking must be resolved before we can delete your account.

9. How we protect it

We use, among other measures: encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest; bcrypt password hashing; short-lived JWT session tokens stored in the device's secure keystore; rate limiting on sensitive endpoints; role-based access so that only administrators can view dispute evidence; and automatic scrubbing of passwords, tokens, and secrets from error reports before they leave our servers.

Access to dispute evidence and handoff photos is restricted to the people who need it to resolve a dispute.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach that creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as PIPEDA requires, and we will keep a record of the breach.

10. Your rights

Under PIPEDA you can:

  • Access — ask what personal information we hold about you, how we have used it, and who we have disclosed it to. We will respond within 30 days. It is free unless the request is unusually costly, in which case we will tell you the cost first.
  • Correct — ask us to fix information that is inaccurate or incomplete. Most of it you can edit yourself in the app.
  • Withdraw consent — see section 4.
  • Delete — see section 8.
  • Complain — write to zoinksupport@gmail.com first. If we do not resolve it to your satisfaction, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca, 1-800-282-1376.

To make a request, email zoinksupport@gmail.com. We may ask you to verify your identity before we act on it.

We may refuse a request in the narrow circumstances PIPEDA allows — for example, where granting access would reveal personal information about another user, or would compromise an active fraud investigation. If we refuse, we will tell you why and tell you how to complain.

11. Automated decisions

Zoink does not make decisions about you by automated means alone that would significantly affect you. Deposit allocations and dispute outcomes are decided by a person who reviews the evidence.

Ratings shown on your profile are calculated automatically from the reviews other users leave.

12. Children

Zoink is for adults. You must be at least 18 to use it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we will delete it and close the account. If you believe a minor has an account, tell us at zoinksupport@gmail.com.

13. Cookies and similar technology

The Zoink mobile app does not use cookies. It stores a session token in your device's secure storage so you stay signed in.

Our landing website (outside the app itself) uses only strictly necessary cookies — the kind needed for the site to function, such as remembering basic technical settings. It does not run analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, and does not use any tool that builds a profile of your visit. If that changes, we will update this section and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change materially affects how we handle your personal information, we will give you notice by email and in the app at least 30 days before it takes effect, and where the law requires it we will ask for your consent. Other changes take effect when posted, and we will update the "Last updated" date.
